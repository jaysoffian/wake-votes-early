Wake Votes Early is an iPhone app that sorts the early voting sites for you in Wake County, NC considering the round-trip drive time from your location and wait time at each site.

How It Works

  • Enter a starting address or use your current location.
  • Every site is ranked by total time. Tap a site's time to get driving directions in Apple Maps; tap the document icon for the county's site details (parking, entrance, accessibility).
  • Wait estimates come from the Wake County Board of Elections and refresh every few minutes. Drive times come from Apple Maps and account for traffic at the time you'd actually be driving.
  • Sites that would be closed by the time you arrive are grayed out and sorted last.

Things to Know

  • Wait estimates are entered by county staff, typically every 30–60 minutes. The app shows how old each one is.
  • Drive times are requested in batches and Apple limits how many can be made per minute. If you change the address or toll setting repeatedly, the app may pause briefly and say so.
  • Early voting hours are the same at every site and are shown at the bottom of the screen.

Contact

Questions, problems, or a site that looks wrong? Open an issue on GitHub. The app's source code is there too.

Wake Votes Early is an independent app and is not affiliated with Wake County or the Wake County Board of Elections. Official early-voting information is at wakevotesearly.com.
