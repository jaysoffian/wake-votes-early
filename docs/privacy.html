Privacy Policy

SupportPrivacy

Effective October 2026.

Wake Votes Early does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of our own. Nothing you enter in the app is sent to the developer.

What stays on your device

  • The starting address you type, and its map coordinates, are saved on your device so you don't have to re-enter them. They are never uploaded anywhere by the app.
  • Your preferences (such as avoiding toll roads) are saved on your device.

Location

If you tap the location button, the app asks for permission to use your location once, to set your starting point. The app does not track your location in the background and does not store a location history. You can use the app without granting location access by typing an address instead.

Services the app talks to

  • Apple Maps. To estimate drive times and show the map, the app sends your starting coordinates and each voting site's coordinates to Apple's MapKit service. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The developer does not see these requests.
  • Wake County Board of Elections. The app downloads the county's public early-voting wait-time data. This request contains no information about you.
  • County site-details documents. If you open a site's details, the app loads a public PDF from the county's website.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
